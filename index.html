The Challenge
The current display rules change session presentations over time—attendees see different content before, during, and after the event. It does not always feature the most engagaing content, nor providing consistency throughout attendee journey. This leads to confusion.
Note: Events are not static. Key engagement drivers—such as live sessions—are only available on the day of the event. So, what should attendees see when they enter the lobby before the event begins? Should their experience differ on the actual event day? And when they return after the event ends, are they looking for a recap of sessions they attended—or catching up on sessions they missed?
How might we create a intuitive lobby experience that works seamlessly across pre-event, live, and post-event states while reducing cognitive load for attendees?

Zoom Events led. Feature the key engagement driver by phase.

Design Option 2

Design Option 3
