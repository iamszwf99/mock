The Challenge
The current display rules dynamically change session presentations over time—attendees see different content before, during, and after the event. This creates confusion and leads to inconsistent user experiences.
Live sessions are only available on the day of the event. So what should attendees see when they enter the lobby before the event begins? Should it differ from what they see on the event day?
How might we create a consistent, intuitive lobby experience that works seamlessly across pre-event, live, and post-event states while reducing cognitive load for attendees?
